Lingaiah Peanut Chutney (Mallepally Tiffin Centre Style)

Andhra-style peanut chutney in the style of the Mallepally Lingaiah tiffin centre — roasted peanuts ground with garlic, red chilli and tamarind, finished with a mustard, cumin and urad dal tempering. Thick, nutty and spicy, served hot with idli.
