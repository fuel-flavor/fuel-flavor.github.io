High-Protein Pumpkin & Cottage Cheese Mini Pancakes

A cozy, autumnal breakfast packed with protein from whole-milk cottage cheese and fiber, vitamin A, and potassium from real pumpkin puree. Blending the cottage cheese directly into the batter creates a silky, melt-in-the-mouth crumb that is easy for toddlers to chew.
