Golden Air-Fryer Tofu "Fries" with Creamy Avocado Dip

A fun, wholesome twist on french fries! Extra-firm tofu is sliced into baton-shaped finger fries, coated in nutritional yeast and cornstarch, and air-fried until golden. Served alongside a creamy dip made of ripe avocado and Greek yogurt.
