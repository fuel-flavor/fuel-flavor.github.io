Mini Chickpea & Sweet Potato Shepherd's Pie Cups

Individual comfort-food cups baked directly in a muffin tin. A rich, savory stew of tender chickpeas, diced carrots, and sweet green peas is spooned into the bottom, then crowned with a pillowy swirl of naturally sweet mashed sweet potatoes. High in plant protein, beta-carotene, and dietary fiber.
