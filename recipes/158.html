High-Protein Granola Bars

Dry-roasted nuts, seeds and oats bound with dates and peanut butter, layered with melted dark chocolate. About 190-205 calories and 9-10g protein per bar (if 6 bars). Store in the fridge; stays good for up to a week. A travel-friendly snack from @greensoverguilt.
