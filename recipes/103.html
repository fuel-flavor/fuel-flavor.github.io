No-Bake Sunflower Butter & Chia Energy Bites

A quick, no-cook snack packed with whole rolled oats, creamy sunflower seed butter (or peanut butter), chia seeds, and ground flaxseed. Sweetened naturally with pure maple syrup or mashed ripe banana (100% honey-free for toddler safety), these bites are rich in healthy fats, fiber, and protein.
