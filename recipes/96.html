Creamy Cauliflower, Cheddar & White Bean "Mac" Shells

A velvety, golden homemade pasta sauce made by blending steamed cauliflower florets with soft cannellini beans, mild cheddar, and a hint of warm butter. Tofu or white beans melt seamlessly into the sauce, boosting protein and fiber while coating mini pasta shells perfectly.
